# ReelVault Members: member guide

ReelVault finds the reels that work best in your niche, shows you why they work, and writes scripts for you
that build on them. This guide walks you through it, step by step.

**What you need**
- A computer with **Chrome, Edge or Brave**. (Phones and Safari or Firefox work for everything except reading
  Instagram.)
- An Instagram account to read with. A second account just for research is safest (see step 3).
- An AI you already have: your Claude plan, or an API key (Gemini has a free one in most countries), or the
  ReelVault desktop app on Windows. You pick in step 4.

Plan about 30 minutes for setup. Your first outliers show up a few minutes after Chrome starts reading.
The full picture (tags, formats, playbooks) takes a few hours. The page tells you how long.

---

## 1. Sign up

1. Open the **ReelVault sign-up link** from the Skool Classroom (or the invite link you got).
2. Type your name, email and a password (at least 10 characters).
3. Type the **community code** from the Classroom. With an invite link you don't need it.
4. Add your Skool profile link if you like. It helps us learn about you.
5. Click **Create my account**.

You may see **"You're on the list"**. That means the admin approves you soon. You get an email if email is set up.
If not, try to log in again later.

Forgot your password? Click **Forgot your password?** on the log-in page. No email comes? Ask the admin for a
reset link.

---

## 2. Setup step 1: your links and 1-2 role models

When you log in the first time, ReelVault walks you through 5 steps. You can skip a step and come back.

1. **Tell us about you.** Paste links that describe you: your Skool profile, your Skool group's About page, your
   website, your LinkedIn, your Instagram. Your website is read right away. Skool, LinkedIn and your Instagram bio are
   read by your own Chrome after step 2. You can also paste text instead.
2. **1-2 Instagram accounts whose reels work in your niche.** Type their @names. We find more like them later.
   No idea yet? Leave it empty. ReelVault can find the top accounts in your niche.
3. **Your own Instagram (optional).** We read your reels too, so you can compare yourself. They never count in your
   competitors' numbers.
4. Click **Save and continue**.

---

## 3. Setup step 2: install and pair the Chrome extension

ReelVault reads Instagram through **your own browser**, with your own login. Your Instagram password and cookies
never leave your browser. ReelVault never posts, likes, follows or sends messages.

### Install the extension

**From the Chrome Web Store** (if you see the button): click **Add to Chrome from the Web Store**, then **Add to
Chrome**.

**From a ZIP file** (if there is no store button yet):
1. Click **Download the extension (ZIP)**.
2. Unzip it into a folder you will keep, for example **Documents/ReelVault extension**. Don't delete this folder later.
3. In Chrome, type **chrome://extensions** in the address bar and press Enter.
   (Edge: **edge://extensions**. Brave: **brave://extensions**.)
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
5. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the folder you unzipped.
6. Pin the extension: click the puzzle piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to
   **ReelVault Members**.

### Pair it with your account

1. In ReelVault, click **Get my pair link**. (Later you find it in **Settings > Chrome extension**.)
2. Click **Copy the link**.
3. Click the **ReelVault Members** icon in Chrome. Paste the link into the box and click **Next**.
4. Check the address it shows. It must be your community's ReelVault address. Click **Connect**.
5. Back in ReelVault you see: **"ReelVault will read Instagram as @yourname."** Check the account.
   Click **Continue**.

The link works for 10 minutes. Only paste links you made yourself in ReelVault. If someone sends you a pair link,
don't use it.

**Not signed in to Instagram?** Open instagram.com in the same Chrome, sign in, then click **Try again** in ReelVault.

### Use a second Instagram account (recommended)

Reading many reels can make Instagram slow your account down for a day. That is rare, because ReelVault reads slowly
and stops when Instagram asks. But if your main account matters to your business, use a second one:
1. In the Instagram app, make a second account (your profile > switch account > **Add account**). Use it normally
   for a few days: follow some accounts in your niche, watch some reels.
2. In Chrome, click your picture at the top right, then **Add**, and make a new Chrome profile called "ReelVault".
3. In that new Chrome window, sign in to Instagram with the second account and install the extension there.
4. Pair it (above). Your main account stays untouched.

### Keep Chrome open

ReelVault reads in a pinned Instagram tab. You can keep using Chrome as normal. If Chrome is closed, reading waits
and starts again when you open Chrome. Reading is slow on purpose: 100 reels take a few minutes.

### Find more accounts

After pairing, step 2 offers:
- **Find more accounts like @your_role_model**, or
- **Find the top accounts in my niche**, or a search box.

ReelVault checks each account's last 12 reels and shows the top 10. Tick the ones to track (up to 10 in total) and
click **Track the ticked accounts**. To keep your Instagram safe, ReelVault reads 5 accounts today and 5 tomorrow.

If you see **Read my links now**, click it. Your Chrome then reads your Skool, LinkedIn and Instagram bio pages.

## 4. Setup step 3: connect your AI

You bring your own AI. ReelVault never picks one for you. Each choice shows a rough estimate of time and cost.
You can change it later in **Settings > Your AI**.

### Option A: Use my Claude plan (the Claude connector)

Your own Claude does the work through our connector. No API key. Any Claude plan works; free plans allow one
custom connector. On Team or Enterprise plans, your Claude owner must add the connector first.

1. In ReelVault, click **Use my Claude plan**. Copy the **connector URL** with the copy button.
2. Open Claude (claude.ai or the Claude desktop app). Go to **Customize > Connectors**.
3. Click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
4. Name: **ReelVault**. URL: paste the connector URL. Click **Continue**, then **Continue** again.
5. Under Authentication, keep **Sign in now** and **Use Claude's published identity**. Click **Add**.
   Don't pick **No sign in**: then ReelVault can't connect.
6. A ReelVault page opens. Log in if it asks, then click **Allow**.
7. Start a new chat in Claude. Click **+**, then **Connectors**, and check that **ReelVault** is on.
8. Say: **Analyze my new ReelVault reels**. When Claude asks to use a ReelVault tool, click **Always allow**.
   (With **Allow once**, Claude asks again at every step.)
9. Back in ReelVault, click **Use my Claude plan** at the bottom of the box, if it is not in use yet.

Claude's screens change a little over time and between accounts. If a button has another name, look for the
closest match. The current steps are always in **Settings > Your AI** in ReelVault.

**Things to say to Claude** (copy buttons are in ReelVault):
- **Analyze my new ReelVault reels**: tags and breaks down your newest reels. Say it again in a new chat when Claude
  stops; it picks up where it left off.
- **Draft my ReelVault profile**: fills in your answers from your links (step 5).
- **Write my ReelVault output out_...**: writes a script, plan or report you asked for. ReelVault shows you the exact
  line to copy.

Good to know:
- Nothing runs until you open Claude. Your first 5 competitors take about 8 Claude chats.
- **Want it faster?** Open **Want it faster? Add a free Gemini key just for tagging** and add a Gemini key (option B).
  Gemini then tags reels on the server, and your Claude still writes your scripts.
- To disconnect, use **Connected Claude apps > Disconnect** in ReelVault, or remove the connector in Claude.
- Claude Code users can add it with the command shown in ReelVault
  (`claude mcp add --transport http --scope user reelvault <connector URL>`).

### Option B: Paste an API key

The AI runs on our server with your key. You pay your AI company for what you use (Gemini has a free tier).

1. Click **Paste an API key**.
2. Pick a provider:
   - **Google Gemini**: a free key, no card, in most countries. Not free in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland.
   - **Anthropic (Claude)**: pick **Claude Sonnet (recommended)**.
   - **OpenAI**.
   - **Other** (OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama Cloud and more): pick the service under **Service**.
3. Click **Get a key from ...**. Sign in there, make a key and copy it.
4. Paste it in **API key** and click **Test and save**. We test it before we save it.

Your key is locked with encryption on our server. ReelVault shows only its last 4 characters. You can delete it any
time. Models marked **can't see images** still work, but visual formats then come from measurements only, so pick a
model that can see images when you can.

### Option C: Use the ReelVault desktop app (Windows)

Claude Code on your Windows computer does the work, on your Claude plan, while the app is open.

1. Click **Use the ReelVault desktop app**, then **Get the desktop app**, and follow the steps there:
   open **PowerShell**, paste the one line shown, press Enter, and sign in to Claude Code when it asks.
2. Open ReelVault from the desktop icon. In **Settings**, find **Use my Claude for ReelVault Members**.
3. Back in ReelVault Members, click **Get a code**. Type the server address and the code into the desktop app and
   click **Link**. The code works for 10 minutes.
4. Click **Use the desktop app**.
5. Keep the desktop app open. It does the AI work while it runs. If you close it, the work waits.

## 5. Setup step 4: 4 quick questions

These are about your **end customer**. Short answers are fine.
1. **Who do you help?** ("I help ___ get ___.")
2. **Their top 3 problems**, in their words.
3. **What do you sell?**
4. **What can you actually make?** (on camera, voice only, screen recording, and so on, plus hours a week and
   whether you have an editor). This decides which formats ReelVault suggests to you.

Click **Fill in my answers from my links** to let your AI pre-fill them. Answers from your links say where they came
from. Check each one.

Click **Review and approve**, read the summary, and click **This is right**. Every script and plan uses this
profile. You can change it any time in **Settings > Profile**. The other 10 questions are optional and make your
scripts better; answer them later.

## 6. Setup step 5: your first look

A few minutes after Chrome reads your first competitor, you see **"Your first look: N outliers from @..."**.
These are the reels that did far better than that creator's normal reel. Tags and formats come later; the page says
when. Click **Go to Home**.

## 7. Add and manage competitors

Open **Competitors** in the left menu.
- **Add:** type @names or paste profile links, one per line, and click **Add and pull 100 reels each**.
  We read the 100 newest reels from each. Private accounts are never read.
- **Find the top accounts in my niche** or **Find accounts like @...**: ReelVault searches from your Chrome and shows
  the best ones to track.
- On a competitor's page:
  - **Pull the next 100 reels** reads 100 older reels.
  - **Check for new reels** reads only what they posted since last time.
  - **Break down the 5 best reels** asks your AI for full breakdowns.
- **Your account**: add your own Instagram to compare yourself with them. It never counts in their numbers.

You can do 5 pulls a day. Extra pulls start the next day by themselves. You can track up to 10 competitors.

**If you see a warning:**
| you see | what to do |
|---|---|
| Waiting for Chrome / Chrome is not open | Open Chrome. Reading starts again. |
| Chrome was signed out of Instagram | Sign in at instagram.com in that Chrome, then click **Try again**. |
| Instagram asked us to slow down | Nothing. ReelVault waits a day, then goes on. |
| Instagram asked for a check | Open instagram.com and follow Instagram's steps. ReelVault waits a day. |
| A new version of the extension is ready | Click **Get the update**. For the ZIP: unzip the new file over the same folder, then click the reload arrow on chrome://extensions. |

## 8. Read outliers

Open **Reels**. Pick **Outliers (3x or more)** to see only the winners.

- **"4.2x normal"** means the reel got 4.2 times the views of that creator's normal reel.
- **Outlier** is 3x or more, **Big outlier** 5x or more, **Breakout** 10x or more.
  **Still growing** means the reel is too new to judge.
- Instagram hides saves, and usually shares, for accounts you don't own. We rank on views, likes and comments.

Click a reel to see:
- **Why it worked** and **What to steal**, once it has a full breakdown. No breakdown yet? Click
  **Get the full breakdown**.
- **Visual format** and its recipe (headline box, captions, face bubble, setting, framing).
- **Transcript**, **Sound cues on the timeline** (click **Play from here**) and **Weak spots** (what you can do better).
- **Wrong tag?** Fix it on the reel page. Your fix wins over the AI.

Sound cues are found by a computer and say "detected, check by ear". Listen before you copy one.

## 9. Read "What works" and the format playbooks

Open **What works**.
- **Formats to copy**: the visual formats that show up more in the outliers than in normal reels. Tick **Only
  formats I can make** to hide what you said you can't film.
- **What makes reels outliers**: hooks, calls to action, sounds, length, cut pace, captions and more.
  "2.8x more likely to be an outlier" means reels with this were 2.8 times as likely to be outliers.
- **Compare one thing at a time**: pick one thing (for example hook type) and **Last 12 months** or **Last 90 days**
  to see what is rising or fading.
- **You vs them**: which formats you use compared with your competitors.
- The tabs **Hooks, Formats, Calls to action, Sounds, Music** are banks you can copy from.

**Format playbooks** are the most useful page. On **Home**, under **Formats that work in your niche**, click a
format. Its page shows:
- **What it is** and **Why it works**,
- **How to shoot it**, **How to edit it** and **The sound**,
- **Can you make this?** (what it needs and the time to make it),
- **Outlier examples** you can open,
- **Write 3 scripts in this format**.

Playbooks appear once enough reels are tagged. Until then Home says so.

## 10. Make scripts, ads, plans and reports

**The fastest way:** on any outlier, reel page, hook or format, click one of these:
- **Remake this**: a script that keeps the reel's format and structure, with your words, angle and proof. It also
  fixes the reel's weak spots.
- **Use this format**: scripts in that format, on your topics.
- **Borrow this hook**: the hook pattern, rewritten for your customer.

**Or open Create** and pick one:
| output | what you get | options |
|---|---|---|
| **Reel scripts** | 1-5 scripts with hook, beats, call to action, caption, an **editor brief** and a **sound cue sheet** | customer problem, goal, what to build on, topic, length |
| **Meta ads** | 15 s and 30 s ad scripts, plus a 5-hook test | how many ad sets, customer problem, goal, what the ad sells |
| **30-day plan** | what to post every day for a month, each day built on a format or outlier | start date, reels a week, carousels and stories too |
| **Competitor report** | what works for them, gaps you can fill, and **the reels to remake first** | which competitors |

Click the button (**Write the scripts**, **Write the ads**, **Make my plan** or **Write the report**). The page fills
in by itself.

- With an API key, it writes on the server in a minute or two.
- With the Claude connector, the page says **Waiting for Claude**. Click **Copy the prompt**, open Claude and paste it.
- With the desktop app, keep the app open.

Your profile must be approved first (setup step 4).

Every output lands in **Outputs**. There you can:
- **Star it**, delete it, or click **Write it again with this change** and say what to change.
- Download it: **Editor brief (.txt)**, **Shot list (.csv)**, **Plan (.csv)**, **Reels to remake (.csv)**,
  **Markdown (.md)** or **Print or save as PDF**.
- **Copy the script** with one click.

If a script line is **Too close to the original**, ReelVault warns you. Change that line before you post.

## 11. Hand the editor brief to an editor

Each reel script comes with everything an editor needs, so you don't have to explain it on a call.

1. Open the script in **Outputs**.
2. Click **Editor brief (.txt)** (or **Print or save as PDF**) and **Shot list (.csv)**.
3. Send both files to your editor with your raw footage.

The brief tells the editor:
- **Format**, **Size and length**, **Pace**, **Captions** and **Look**.
- **Music**: type, mood, speed and volume, with track ideas (or "none, voice only").
- **B-roll to find or film**, **Graphics** and **Proof to show** (your results, screenshots, testimonials).
- **Do** and **Don't**.
- **Watch these moments**: links to the original outlier on Instagram with the exact seconds to copy the style from.

The **Sound cue sheet** lists every sound: when, what, why, how loud, and where to get it (with an example from the
outlier). Your editor follows it second by second.

Tip: tell your editor to copy the *style* of the reference moments, never the clips themselves.

## 12. Your account and privacy

- **Settings > Account**: change your password, download all your data (ZIP), or delete your account.
- Only you see your data. Other members never can. The admin can look only if you turn on **Let the admin see your
  account** for a few days when you ask for help. You see when they looked.
- We never keep your Instagram password or cookies, or your Claude login.
- Stuck? Open **Help** in the menu, or ask in the community.
